# GPX export checklist

AllTrails only creates a GPX download when you click Export yourself (it needs a one-time reCAPTCHA token), so these are links to each recording page, not direct download links.

**For each row:** click the link → the `...` (More options) button at the top of the left panel → *Export route file* → *Export* (format *GPX Track*). Files land in `~/Downloads`. When you're done, tell Claude and it will match, rename, copy them into `gpx/` and convert them. Tip: pause a few seconds between exports.

Row numbers match `n` in `src/lab/activities.json`. Titles on AllTrails sometimes differ slightly from the activity titles.
